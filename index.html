How to mix a vial

Draw up the bacteriostatic water, push it in slowly down the inside wall of the vial so it does not spray the powder, then swirl or roll gently. Never shake. Refrigerate after mixing (2–8 °C) and use within the window shown for that peptide, usually about 4 weeks. Check the vial's capacity before adding 3 mL or more; most vendor vials are 3 mL.

units to draw = dose ÷ vial size × mL of water × 100 (same unit for dose and vial size)

Install on your phone

It opens like an app from your home screen and keeps working without a signal. Your vials and dose log stay on this device.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of Safari; if you do not see it, tap ··· first).
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

The installed iPhone app keeps its own saved data, separate from Safari. Install first, then add your vials inside the app.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen), then Install.

On a computer, Chrome and Edge show an install icon at the right end of the address bar.

This page looks like it opened inside another app (Facebook, Instagram, an email or chat app). Use that app's menu to choose Open in Safari or Open in Chrome first. Installing does not work from inside other apps.
